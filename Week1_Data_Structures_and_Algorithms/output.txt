After Bubble Sort (O(n²)):
  Order #4 | Diana        | $75.25
  Order #2 | Bob          | $150.75
  Order #1 | Alice        | $250.00
  Order #5 | Eve          | $320.00
  Order #3 | Charlie      | $500.50

After Quick Sort (O(n log n)):
  Order #4 | Diana        | $75.25
  Order #2 | Bob          | $150.75
  Order #1 | Alice        | $250.00
  Order #5 | Eve          | $320.00
  Order #3 | Charlie      | $500.50
Time: 2500 ns

--- Performance Comparison ---
Bubble Sort: O(n²) - Simple but slow for large datasets
Quick Sort:  O(n log n) avg - Much faster, preferred in practice
